The Room That Remembers

An interactive memory, in three objects.

You haven't been here in a long time. The light is the colour of late afternoon even though it shouldn't be. Three things in this room are waiting for you to notice them. There is no rush. There is no wrong order.

Touch each object, in any order. Each one will give you a word.

The room gives you a word.

You stand in the centre of the room. Outside, the city is doing what it always does. Inside, the air tastes of old paper and dust and something almost like welcome. You whisper it to no one, and to yourself, and to whoever lived here before you forgot you lived here too:

I ______ what I ______,
and ______ what I could not say.
